FitSize Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Developer: FitSize, independent developer · Contact: yueyang295513@gmail.com

In short: FitSize processes videos on your iPhone or iPad. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking. Your videos and personal information are never sent to the developer.

What FitSize processes

  • The videos you choose. Videos can come from the system Photos picker, the Files app, the Photos share sheet, or Shortcuts. FitSize reads the picture, sound, duration, dimensions, frame rate, and file size to show a preview, work out the size budget, and export. All of this happens on your device.
  • Location and time stored in a video. Some videos include where and when they were recorded. In the app's workbench, the Photos share sheet, and Shortcuts, an exported MP4 keeps any location coordinates and capture date FitSize recognizes by default, and saving to Photos uses the original recording time as the date. You can turn on Remove Location to drop the coordinates; the capture date is still kept. The Remove Video Location shortcut rewraps the file without re-encoding the picture or sound. FitSize never reads your device's current location.

Photos access

  • When you use the Photos picker, iOS hands FitSize only the items you select. No Photos permission is needed.
  • The Compress Latest Recording shortcut asks to read Photos and looks only for the newest screen recording among the photos you allow.
  • When you tap Save to Photos, FitSize asks for add-only access so it can save the file you chose.
  • If a video is stored only in iCloud Photos, iOS may download it first.
  • You can change these permissions in iOS Settings at any time, or pick videos from Files instead.

What FitSize doesn't do

  • It doesn't upload your videos, exports, file names, locations, or usage.
  • It has no ads, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs, and doesn't track you across apps or websites.
  • The app makes no requests to developer servers. The only network activity comes from iOS itself: App Store purchases and restores, downloading a video you picked from iCloud, and the online privacy policy link if you tap it.

Data kept on your device

  • Working copies. FitSize makes copies in its temporary folder while importing and exporting, and deletes them when they're no longer needed. iOS also clears this folder.
  • Waiting videos. If you tap Continue in FitSize in the Photos share sheet, or the video is too long or large for the panel, a copy and its settings (size limit, mute, remove location, and so on) are kept in a container on your device that the app and its share extension both use. The copy is deleted once you continue in the app or remove it. Partial files left by an interrupted handoff are cleared the next time the app checks the waiting list, once they're over 7 days old. Your original video is not touched.
  • Purchase status. So the app, share extension, and Shortcuts all recognize the same purchase, FitSize stores the product ID, transaction ID, purchase and signing dates, whether it was revoked, when it was last checked, and how long the check is valid. This contains no name, Apple Account, or payment details.
  • Your Home Screen icon choice is stored by iOS.
  • Deleting FitSize removes all of the above. Files you saved to Photos or Files, or shared elsewhere, are managed in those places. The developer has no server holding your media, so there is nothing to look up or delete on your behalf.

Purchases and refunds

The paid download and the one-time FitSize Pro purchase are handled by Apple's App Store. FitSize checks transactions with Apple's StoreKit and never sees your payment details. Apple provides the developer with sales reports, as described in Apple's privacy policy. Refunds are handled by Apple.

When you contact support

If you email yueyang295513@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, your message, and anything you attach. This is used only to reply and fix the problem. It isn't used for marketing or shared with anyone. The mailbox is provided by Gmail (Google) and messages are stored there. You can ask for your messages to be deleted at any time. There's no need to send videos, your Apple Account password, or payment details.
