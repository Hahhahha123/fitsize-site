FAQ

How do I get a video under 20 MB?

In Compare, tap Change Video and pick a video from Photos or Files. Drag the trim handles to keep the part you need, then tap Compress to 20 MB · MP4. When it's done, drag the divider to compare the picture. The result screen shows the exact byte count, and you can save to Photos, save to Files, or share.

Why won't my video fit the limit?

With a fixed limit, the longer the video, the less data each second gets. If a clip can't fit even at the lowest quality, FitSize tells you instead of exporting a file over the limit. Your original isn't changed. Try one of these:

  • Trim the clip, or use Last 6 s or Last 30 s under Fine-Tune.
  • Turn on Mute so the budget goes to the picture.
  • Turn on Max 30 fps, or choose Sharp as the priority.
  • With FitSize Pro, raise the limit, for example to 50 MB.

If the original is already under the limit, the workbench shows Already Under Limit · Share Original, so you can skip compression.

FitSize isn't in the Photos share sheet

In Photos, select a video and tap Share. Look for FitSize in the app row. Some iOS versions show it as FitSize Compress. If it's missing, scroll to the end, tap More, and add FitSize.

How do I continue after sharing from Photos?

For short videos, tap Compress Here in the share panel, then save to Files or share. For the full app, tap Continue in FitSize, open FitSize, go to Tools → Waiting Videos, and tap Continue. Longer or larger videos go straight to the waiting list. You can remove waiting videos on the same screen. Your original stays where it is.

Compress Latest Recording can't find my recording

This shortcut only looks at photos FitSize is allowed to access. Check FitSize under Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos, or use the Compress Video shortcut with a file instead.

I can't save to Photos

Allow FitSize to add photos in iOS Settings, or tap Save to Files on the result screen.

How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought FitSize Pro with. Open FitSize, go to Tools → Basic & Pro, and tap Restore Purchases. If it says nothing was found, check that you're using the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

FitSize Pro is sold by Apple, so Apple handles refunds. The developer can't issue them directly. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple Account you used, find FitSize, and request a refund. If Apple refunds FitSize Pro, Pro features turn off the next time FitSize checks your purchase.

Does FitSize upload my videos?

No. Compression happens on your device. FitSize has no accounts and no servers of its own. See the Privacy Policy.
